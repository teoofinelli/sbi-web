01 — What we do

From real agricultural needs to technologies that solve them.

SBI identifies needs and market opportunities in agriculture, then connects them with technologies from around the world that are able to address them — with an initial focus on biosolutions and Latin America.

A

We start from the problem

We identify real needs, the limits of today's solutions and the opportunities they open for growers and agri-food chains.

B

We find the technology

We search globally for technologies, innovations and partners that respond to those needs — well beyond traditional markets.

C

We take it to market

We assess local fit, evidence, scalability, regulation and time to market, and help build the path to commercial entry.

We are not a distributor or a catalogue. We first find where the gap is — then the technology it needs.

02 — Our approach

We start with the problem, not with a product to sell.

Five stages take an opportunity from a real need in the field to a viable commercial solution.

  1. 01

    Real Needs

    Understand the problems and demands of agriculture, crop by crop and market by market.

  2. 02

    Market Fit

    Identify gaps and opportunities where current solutions don't respond well enough.

  3. 03

    Technology Scouting

    Search globally for technologies capable of solving those problems.

  4. 04

    Validation

    Assess differentiation, applicability, scalability and potential for local adaptation.

  5. 05

    Market Development

    Help turn the opportunity into a viable commercial solution.

03 — What we look for

Technologies with real fit and the potential to scale.

It isn't about adding products. We look for differentiated solutions to relevant problems.

Agronomic value

Consistent efficacy, measurable benefit and adaptation to real production conditions.

Differentiation

A concrete gap: a poorly solved need, a limitation in today's offer or an emerging opportunity.

Business viability

Scalable manufacturing, a clear regulatory route, a reliable partner and a reasonable time to market.

Selection filters

  1. Real need
  2. Technological edge
  3. Evidence
  4. Scalability
  5. Regulation
  6. Market

04 — How we think
